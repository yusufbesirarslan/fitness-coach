FOUNDATION MOVEMENT: goblet squat, incline push-up, row, dumbbell RDL, dead bug.
STABILITY + UNILATERAL: split squat, dumbbell shoulder press, pulldown, glute bridge, Pallof press.
CONDITIONING CIRCUIT: squat to press, swing or hinge drill, mountain climber, push-up, walking lunge.
MOVEMENT PATTERNS: squat, hinge, push, pull, unilateral, anti-rotation core.
PROGRESSION: add reps, tempo control, stability, then load.
EDGE CASES: beginners use hinge drills instead of swings; low conditioning gets fewer rounds and longer rest.
