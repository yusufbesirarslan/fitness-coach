PUSH DAY: bench press, incline dumbbell press, fly/pec deck, lateral raise, triceps pushdown, overhead triceps extension.
PULL DAY: pulldown/pull-up, row, seated row, rear delt fly, curl, hammer curl.
LEG DAY: squat or hack squat, leg press, Romanian deadlift, leg curl, leg extension, calf raise.
HYPERTROPHY: beginner 8-12 sets/muscle/week, intermediate 12-18, advanced 16-24 only if recovery allows.
FAILURE POLICY: no compound failure; isolation can approach failure for advanced users.
FATIGUE: manage chest/shoulder/triceps overlap and high systemic fatigue after legs.
