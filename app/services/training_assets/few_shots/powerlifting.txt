SQUAT FOCUS: back squat 5x3-5 RPE 7-9, paused squat 3x3-4, lower accessory, core stability.
BENCH HEAVY: bench press 5x3-5, close grip bench, upper-back row, triceps isolation.
DEADLIFT DAY: deadlift 3-5x2-5, RDL, pulldown or pull-up, hamstring isolation.
BENCH VOLUME: bench 4x6-10, incline dumbbell press, lateral raise, seated row.
PROGRESSION: double progression, upper lifts +2.5-5%, lower lifts up to +5% when RPE allows.
FATIGUE: do not peak squat and deadlift together; deload every 4-6 weeks.
