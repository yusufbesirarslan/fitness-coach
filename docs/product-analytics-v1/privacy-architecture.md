# Privacy gates and proposed architecture

## Destination decision

Recommend **PostHog EU Cloud conditionally**, after vendor/privacy clearance, because its product funnels/cohorts avoid building a broad analytics platform for 100 users. Prefer Flutter -> typed facade -> first-party authenticated analytics gateway -> approved destination, with backend post-commit authoritative events entering the same validator. This preserves a destination switch and a single independent consent gate. It adds no vendor SDK to the pilot binary. A direct Flutter -> vendor adapter remains possible only after separate SDK and metadata inspection; not recommended for initial launch.

PostHog documents EU Cloud storage in Frankfurt and an EU ingestion host. This is not proof that every processing/access path stays in the EU. Its current subprocessor list includes global Cloudflare transit and must be evaluated with the DPA and internal/support access. Disable vendor AI, assistants, integrations, surveys, advertising/experiments, replay and autocapture for this project. No user content for classification. No price/retention-plan assumptions: founder procurement must verify current account limits before any purchase.

First-party fallback: same typed events and authority; separately approved EU event table with timestamp partition/TTL and uniqueness, restricted server mapping, read-only aggregate queries rendered as four reports. Small first-party implementation avoids another processor but still needs legal basis, access controls, security/backup/deletion operations and an explicitly approved migration. Reusing application sensitive tables or CloudWatch per-account AI logs is not this fallback. Neither storage nor a PostHog project is provisioned in these drafts.

## Authorization and collection

Analytics is optional. A separate versioned purpose `product_analytics` has its own affirmative grant, refusal, withdrawal and generation; default unknown/declined means zero collection, buffering or SDK initialization. Fail closed on authority or consent lookup failure. Health-processing, AI/Bedrock transfer, login, terms acceptance, paid status, landing cookies and ATT never substitute for it. Declining analytics must not affect Coach or any other entitlement. Do not emit person-level refusal events.

Proposed EN copy, **draft requiring legal/localization review**: “Help improve AxisAI by sharing basic app use, such as screens opened and whether features worked. We do not send your messages, photos, food contents or health measurements for analytics. This is optional. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.” Equal “Allow analytics” / “Not now”; no preselected toggle. Show destination, retention and privacy-choice links in reviewed EN/TR detail. Do not ship placeholder copy or prompts in build 101119.

Initial pilot asks after authentication, before onboarding, with a first-party authority holding evidence (purpose/version/text digest/decision time/generation) independently of product events. Accounts already onboarded enroll as existing_user. Complete signup funnel collection is unavailable until a separate pre-auth opt-in flow is reviewed; no anonymous device tracking fills that gap. A future pre-auth enrollment would require a short-lived independent consent capability, principal binding at verified signup and abuse/rate-limit review, never ordinary app auth tokens sent to a vendor.

Legal owner must document applicable GDPR/KVKK/ePrivacy/device-storage rules, purpose and necessity, optional consent/lawful basis, withdrawal, age eligibility and whether coarse feature use could infer health and trigger additional sensitive-data obligations. Even content-free fitness behavior can be sensitive. Any health inference requires a documented lawful decision or exclusion, not a claim that pseudonymization removes the issue. Assess DPIA necessity; record controller/processor roles, recipients, retention, DPA, international transfer safeguards and applicable Turkish notification duties. EU hosting alone is not clearance. No silent fallback to legitimate interest if consent is refused.

## Delivery, identity and lifecycle

Source prototype: 100 memory-pending events per current grant, dedup cap 1024, TTL 24h, total three attempts with 10s then 30s backoff, no polling/background timer/disk queue. No SDK, real transport, source import, thread or scheduler is installed. `record` performs no network IO. `drain` is manually driven in tests; a real sink must set timeouts. No event on cold start before a grant; events lost on exit are not replayed from sensitive functional records.

Future delivery limits: foreground only, at most 20 events or 40 KiB per batch, minimum 60 seconds between automatic flushes (separate fixed aggregate quality counters may record drops), one request in flight, 2s connect/3s total timeout, bounded jittered backoff and three total attempts per event, 24h TTL, no battery-expensive reachability polling/background job. Retry only connection/timeout/429/5xx with capped Retry-After; discard other 4xx. Quality counters contain fixed failure enums only, no payload or subject. These are release requirements, not claims about an unimplemented transport. Require measured startup/scroll/network overhead on a physical device; analytics must never await or change a critical application result.

Future gateway derives subject from authenticated account + current independent grant; strips IP, user-agent, forwarded headers, referrer/URL and all unapproved metadata before forwarding. Configure edge/access logs not to retain analytics request bodies/auth headers or attach IP to event rows. Review reverse proxy security logs/retention separately. No person profiles, identity aliasing or `$set` traits at vendor. Approved adapter maps stable event_id to destination dedup field, sends exact occurred_at and forces GeoIP disabled; verify actual vendor payload/results with synthetic fixtures. Do not rely on EU SDK defaults.

Production consent withdrawal must serialize authorization and dispatch across workers, revoke generation immediately, cancel queued/in-flight-unsent work on all devices, invalidate enrollment capability and trigger deletion. The prototype checks grant at record and drain and clears on change; it does not implement distributed serialization. A vendor request already sent cannot be recalled; deletion must cover it after in-flight dispatch has settled, with repeat sweep/retry. Gateway refuses stale grant even for offline queues. Do not confirm completion of withdrawal/erasure prematurely.

Logout: clear local queue/subject/dedup before returning to signed-out UI. Account switch: discard old events, never relabel them under a new subject, including a delayed callback/network response. Delayed callbacks must carry the initiating analytics generation and be dropped if it changed; the prototype does not wire any callbacks. Never ask current user identity for an old operation. Account deletion: authority retires mapping and generation first, cancels producers/outbox, requests deletion for every historical grant's subject in first-party and vendor sinks; verify completion and preserve a restricted erasure audit until resolved. Never reuse a deleted subject. Withdrawal policy for launch: stop and request erasure of prior optional analytics as well; AI/health/account data are governed independently. Health withdrawal must also stop any overlapping product activity inference until reviewed permission policy is satisfied.

Proposed retention: raw product events at most 90 days, enough for D30 maturation; identity mapping only while grant/events exist; consent evidence under separately approved accountability schedule, never indefinite by accident. First-party expiry job daily and backups expire within an approved additional maximum 30 days with restoration erasure replay. PostHog retention may clean up monthly rather than at exact age; require verified contractual/configured maximum including cleanup lag <=90 days, or run explicit erasure before the bound. If vendor plan cannot satisfy this, use fallback or revise notice/retention only after approval. A nominal “90 day” UI setting alone is insufficient. Only approved aggregate reports without identifiers and with small-cell protection may be kept up to 12 months. Recompute reports after erasure where re-identification remains possible. No anonymous permanence claim for small cohorts.

## App Store and notice

Before future activation, approved EN/TR Privacy Notice must name optional analytics purpose, data fields/pseudonymous account identifier, recipient PostHog (if selected), processing/transfer locations and safeguards, retention, rights/withdrawal/deletion path, and content exclusions. Update in-app privacy choices and support route. Backend analytics alone still changes the privacy practice if it uses app-originated data; absence of an SDK does not exempt disclosure.

Expected Apple disclosure delta, subject to review of actual payload: Identifiers -> User ID (pseudonymous account-linked), Usage Data -> Product Interaction, purpose Analytics, linked to user because AxisAI retains a mapping. Consider Fitness/Health only if approved event semantics actually imply those categories; do not claim content-free events automatically avoid them. No diagnostic/device identifier categories unless newly collected. No Data Used to Track You under this design: no cross-company advertising linkage or broker sharing. ATT is a separate Apple tracking test, not an analytics consent mechanism; reassess if a vendor changes use. Include SDK privacy manifests/signatures/required-reason API review only if a future SDK is introduced; validate the final Xcode privacy report regardless. A privacy manifest does not replace App Store labels or legal notices.

Apple allows privacy-answer updates without a binary update; our new consent UI/facade integration needs a separately qualified future mobile binary. Neither is authorized here. Keep 101119 and its review submission unchanged. Founder approval required before any ASC edits, build upload, rollout, source merge or collection flag enablement.

## Clearance evidence before activation

1. Founder/privacy owner approves catalog, consent UI/versioned evidence, lawful basis, sensitivity assessment, recipient/transfer decision and notices.
2. Founder separately authorizes storage/authority migration and implementation; authority, authenticated ingress, durable receipt/dedup and erasure integrate with account lifecycle.
3. Synthetic staging tests prove refusal/withdrawal/concurrent dispatch, cross-device logout/account isolation, unknown-field rejection, exactly bounded metadata, multi-worker replay uniqueness, skew/late delivery, retries/429/5xx/4xx, deletion including in-flight/backups and TTL. No real user events during qualification.
4. Test device captures show zero destination traffic before consent/after refusal; only approved fields after synthetic grant; no startup, Coach, auth or workout regression. Vendor retention/deletion are verified, not assumed.
5. Founder separately approves source merge, future release/App Store disclosure changes, and an explicit small authorized opt-in pilot enablement with rollback/kill switch. Kill switch stops producer, gateway and sink, clears queues; operational metrics remain unchanged.

## Primary sources reviewed 2026-11-19

- [PostHog storage and deletion](https://posthog.com/docs/privacy/data-storage): Frankfurt option, asynchronous deletion and retention cleanup behavior.
- [PostHog API hosts](https://posthog.com/docs/api): EU host separation; verify exact API/version again during adapter implementation.
- [PostHog Flutter](https://posthog.com/docs/libraries/flutter), [autocapture](https://posthog.com/docs/product-analytics/autocapture): auto/lifecycle/offline features exist; never initialize by default.
- [PostHog DPA](https://posthog.com/dpa), [subprocessors](https://posthog.com/subprocessors): contract and processing/transfer review required. Subprocessor page updated November 6, 2026 includes worldwide transit.
- [Apple App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/), [privacy and data use](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/): data types, identity linkage, manifest and ATT distinctions.
- [GDPR regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/pdf), [KVKK law incl. amended Article 9](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6649/Personal-Data-Protection-Law): legal review inputs; applicable safeguards and duties must be confirmed for AxisAI's actual processing.
