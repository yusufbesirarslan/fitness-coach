Existing mobile `docs/mobile_analytics_v1.md` and parts of `mobile_privacy_inventory.md` predate shipped signup, onboarding, live Coach, progress and subscriptions. Runtime source and tests take precedence. The new proposal supersedes the old future-Firebase example and per-install identity suggestion for this task only; it does not change active behavior.

Privacy Notice text appears to be managed separately from the backend templates. The exact published EN/TR notice and current App Store answers were not read or modified; retrieving their approved versions is a clearance gate. Source health/AI disclosures describe their specific purposes, not product measurement.
