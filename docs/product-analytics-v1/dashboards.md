# Launch dashboard and weekly review specification

All dashboards use schema v1, separate production/staging projects, Europe/Istanbul dates, UTC event instants, exact-day retention, 24h ingestion watermark, independent valid analytics grants and the eligibility rules in `events-kpis.md`. Exclude internal/test enrollment at the authority. No raw content, person drilldowns, replay, email lists or joins to AI usage logs. Show `x/n`, date window, cohort maturity, eligible n, exclusions/unknowns and collection coverage on every page. Suppress sliced rates/counts with fewer than five distinct contributors and hide totals/complementary slices that allow subtraction to recover a suppressed cell; founder restricted top-level funnel totals may be displayed under the approved access policy. Mark n<10 directional. Small-cohort output is still governed data.

## 1. Signup and activation funnel

Top row: independently reviewed aggregate registration total if later authorized; analytics new_user/existing_user enrollment counts; activation eligible n and collection uptime. Main funnel: enrollment -> onboarding_started -> onboarding_completed -> training_plan_requested / succeeded -> coach_interaction_requested / succeeded -> activated-within-7d. Show an ordered exploration for onboarding/plan/Coach navigation, but compute activation in any order; do not force sequential funnel settings to redefine the KPI. Separate intent-to-success rates by action_id, lost-response client failure vs committed success, and first-plan/Coach time-to-value distributions with non-success denominators.

Signup_started -> signup_completed within 24h is a reserved panel: N/A until approved pre-auth opt-in exists. Current after-auth enrollment cannot produce a reliable whole signup funnel. Never use app_opened as signup intent or show the entire first 100 as its denominator. Onboarding no-observed-completion by coarse step after 24h+watermark; censor permission withdrawal/outages. Actions: high start/low confirmed complete -> inspect the relevant functional error path with existing operational diagnostics; low plan-to-Coach adoption -> review introduction/placement without changing AI permissions.

## 2. Screen navigation and feature adoption

Panels: allowlisted screen reach by distinct consenting subjects / eligible enrolled subjects in same complete period; count views separately from users. Transition matrix of consecutive visible allowlisted screens per authorized foreground session; do not fabricate order across sessions/devices (route order uses local dispatcher order, ambiguous equal timestamps excluded). Device session keys remain memory-only and are not exported in v1: until a reviewed sequence field exists, cross-screen transitions use unambiguous single-device timestamp order only and are labeled incomplete; reach/adoption are primary. All nutrition subroutes collapse to nutrition; exclude sensitive/result routes entirely.

Show first plan, Coach, completed workout, food log and nutrition plan adoption within 7d; first adoption vs repeat use. Show unique workout-start subjects and unique completion/explicit-abandonment subjects separately. Do not claim per-workout conversion: start and completion are distinct actions in v1; exact lifecycle joins require a separately reviewed random lifecycle identifier/contract extension. Cohort eligibility matters: no “unused” inference from absent consent. Failure rates and observed recovery per feature, with client/server panels separate. Actions: high screen reach/low success -> fix completion path; low reach/high success -> examine navigation; repeats after failure -> prioritize recovery UX. No food-method, health-goal or injury segments.

## 3. Retention and reactivation

Heatmap by local enrollment date/week: D1/D7/D30 exact-date meaningful retention with eligible denominator n, finalized/immature/censored state and data-quality coverage. Existing_user and activated-date retention in separate panels. DAU/WAU trend based on meaningful success set only. Inactive7/inactive30 counts and rates at each week start; fixed week-start inactive7 cohort reactivation x/n, plus newly-inactive returners as a separate count. Never convert missing days during disabled collection to churn. D30 appears only after target date ends plus 24h; 90-day raw retention bounds historical reconstructability.

Actions: low activation/high activated retention -> onboarding priority; high activation/low D7 -> investigate repeat value/workflow friction; apparent retention drop coincident with grant/ingestion outage -> repair measurement before changing product. No contact exports or automated re-engagement from these cohorts.

## 4. Coach engagement and aggregate topics

Panels: distinct Coach-success subjects / eligible enrolled subjects; first successful reply latency from consented enrollment; repeat meaningful Coach use on >=2 distinct local days/week; authoritative success/failure by day; recovery after visible failure; normal completed reply vs provider fallback distinguished. No turn counts from provider attempts. Label success as response completed, not evidence that advice worked or user read it. No prompts/responses/tools arguments/refusal categories/medical topics/health segments.

Topic panel has a separate blocked aggregate specification. No per-user topic event or property is in catalog.json. Proposed buckets: training, nutrition, app_help, other. Only reviewed existing structured entrypoint/tool-operation metadata may supply a coarse tag; never `coach_privacy.operation(question)`, a new message classifier or an LLM summary. Unknown metadata -> other. Deduplicate by authorized successful logical turn; aggregate only inside first-party boundary after independent analytics authorization. Require >=5 distinct authorized contributors per bucket/week, coarsen/suppress rare buckets, and suppress complementary totals/slices that allow differencing. Export only `{week_start, topic, interaction_count}` after threshold validation, with no subject/action/timestamps finer than week. Internal transient distinct-contributor sets are governed sensitive pseudonymous data with immediate bounded expiry and erasure reconciliation. No user-segment joins or drilldown, and do not calculate percentage from a denominator containing suppressed counts. All use of the structured tag and even broad nutrition/training inference requires privacy-owner approval. Until a metadata source and legal gate pass, show “Unavailable: aggregate topic review pending”, never extract old conversations.

## Weekly founder review (Monday after 24h watermark)

One page, manually reviewed, no automatic messages sent. Reporting week Monday 00:00 through next Monday 00:00 Europe/Istanbul; finalize after Tuesday 00:00. Include:

1. Measurement status: enabled/disabled, builds, watermark, enrolled new/existing n, gaps/censored counts, no-consent coverage limits, erasure/retention status. While HOLD, say “No product collection; baseline unavailable”.
2. Activation x/n and biggest observed funnel loss; plan/Coach time-to-value and failures with denominator. One concrete fix hypothesis and owner.
3. Meaningful WAU, feature adoption x/n, completed workouts and repeat Coach-use users; no exercise/food/body values. Separate reach from confirmed value.
4. Mature D1/D7/D30 x/n, week-start inactive7 reactivation x/n, previous comparable mature cohort only. Mark immature/small/coverage-limited explicitly.
5. Coarse Coach topics only if independently cleared and thresholds met; otherwise blocked. Never quote messages as analytics evidence.
6. At most three prioritized actions with owner, expected observable outcome, due date and next-week check. Examples: start intent high and success low -> fix confirmed error/recovery path; success stable but repeat WAU low -> test a navigation/return-value improvement with separate authorization. No experiment deployment is implied.

Use two comparable mature weeks before interpreting a directional change; do not treat the first 100 as statistically representative or set fabricated performance targets. Founder review is a specification, not an authorization to email, publish or activate anything.
